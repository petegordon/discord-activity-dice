Privacy Policy

7, 11, Doubles · Effective October 15, 2026

7, 11, Doubles (the "Game") is a dice game you can play as a Discord Activity or on the web at dice.housetandem.com. It is provided by Users First LLC together with HouseTandem ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what information the Game uses, why, and who can see it.

The short version: we collect only what the Game needs to run a table. We don't run ads or analytics, we don't set tracking cookies, we don't sell or rent your information, and we don't keep a database of players.

1. Information we use

InformationWhere it comes fromWhat it's used for
Discord username, display name and avatar Discord, when you launch the Activity and approve the identify permission Showing who is at the table. We don't request your email, friends, servers or messages.
Name you type (web version) You Showing who is at the table. It's saved in your browser so you don't have to retype it.

IP address and basic request data (browser type, time) Your device's network connection Delivering the Game, connecting players, and protecting the service from abuse. See section 3.

2. Who can see it

  • Other players at your table see your display name, avatar, player color, throws, dice results and scores.
  • Peer-to-peer play: the web version connects players directly using WebRTC by default. In that mode, the other players' devices can see your IP address, as with any peer-to-peer connection. Discord Activities use our relay server by default, so players don't see each other's IP addresses.
  • We don't share your information with advertisers or data brokers, and we never sell it.

3. Service providers

These services process limited information so the Game can work:

  • Discord: provides sign-in, your profile name and avatar, and avatar images inside the Activity. Discord's own Privacy Policy applies to your use of Discord.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the Game and runs our server and relay. Cloudflare processes request data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the service. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • PeerJS (peer-to-peer mode only): a public connection server (0.peerjs.com) helps players' devices find each other. It sees your IP address and your random session ID.
  • STUN servers operated by Cloudflare and Google (peer-to-peer mode only): help devices discover how to reach each other. They see your IP address.

4. How long we keep it

  • Table state (players, scores, recent rolls) is held in memory only while a table is active. Our relay doesn't write it to a database, and it's gone once everyone leaves.
  • Your browser stores your typed name, your sound on/off setting and, if you calibrate your throw, a few numbers describing your throwing style (in localStorage) until you clear your browser data. These stay on your device.
  • A phone used as a dice cup keeps its remote key in the browser tab's sessionStorage, so a refresh doesn't disconnect it. It's deleted when you close the tab.
  • Our hosting provider may keep standard request and security logs for a limited time under its own policies.

5. Cookies and tracking

The Game doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies or tracking pixels. It uses your browser's storage only for the preferences, throw calibration and phone remote key described above.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can play the web version without a Discord account, under any name you choose.
  • You can decline motion access. The Game then lets you throw with Quick roll, or by flicking your finger on a phone remote.
  • You can clear your saved name and settings at any time by clearing your browser's site data.
  • You can revoke the Game's Discord access in Discord under User Settings → Authorized Apps.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. We don't keep player records, but if you have a request or question, email us at games@usersfirst.com and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children

The Game isn't directed to children under 13, and you must meet Discord's minimum age (or the minimum age in your country, if higher) to use it as a Discord Activity. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll address it.

8. Security

Connections to the Game use HTTPS or encrypted WebRTC. Session keys stop other players from joining as you. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we keep what we handle to a minimum.

9. International users

The Game is operated from the United States, and our service providers may process information in other countries. By using the Game, you understand that your information may be processed outside your country of residence.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the effective date. If the changes are significant, we'll take reasonable steps to let players know, such as a notice in the Game.
